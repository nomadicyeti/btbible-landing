Meet Anu

Meet the sister who synchronized the audio and text in the Dzongkha Bible

Hello my name is Anu and I am from Nepal. I’m so grateful for the opportunity to be part of the Audio Bible project and to see it completed. I really enjoyed helping with the time alignment. It felt like sowing seeds and I’m excited to see how God will use His Word among the Bhutanese people.

I am married to a man from an ethnic Tibetan tribe (Lho-mi) and my husband (Tenzing Lhomi) and I were blessed to visit Bhutan last December. It was a beautiful experience and I felt so much at home. We were inspired by the people and their eagerness to serve the Lord. We also had the opportunity to meet Bhutanese believers and churches and share that the people of Bhutan are chosen and loved by God.

My husband and I have a heart to serve the Tibetan Buddhist people. It is a great privilege to be part of what God is doing among these people groups.

We are excited to see how God will work through this. Please let us know how we can pray for you guys. Bhutan is always in our hearts and prayers. 🙏🤍
